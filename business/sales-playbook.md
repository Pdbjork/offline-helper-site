# Offline Helper Sales Playbook

## Positioning

Offline Helper is not “AI consulting.” It is patient, done-for-you setup for families who want a useful local-first AI helper without becoming technical.

Core promise:

> “We make AI feel safe, private, and understandable on your own Mac or Windows PC — fit-check first, no pressure.”

## Fit-check script

1. Who is this for?
2. What computer do they use? Mac/Windows, approximate year, available storage/RAM if known.
3. What are the first 1–3 useful jobs?
4. What feels risky or confusing?
5. Who needs to understand or approve the setup?
6. What would make the setup clearly worth it after one week?

## Good-fit signals

- Clear first workflow: writing, family notes, organizing, learning, tech Q&A.
- Customer values privacy and patient explanation.
- Device is recent enough for local or local-first setup.
- Family organizer can help with remote session if needed.
- Customer understands this is not medical/legal/financial/crisis support.

## Not-fit signals

- Wants guaranteed fully offline use with phone/SMS/cloud integrations.
- Needs emergency, medical, legal, financial, or regulated advice.
- Expects autonomous account control, password handling, or secret management.
- Device is too old and customer does not want alternatives.
- Family consent is unclear.

## Close language

If fit:

> “This looks like a good Starter/Family Setup fit. The next step is a scheduled setup session. I’ll send checkout only now that we know what we’re setting up and what boundaries matter.”

If not fit:

> “I don’t think taking payment would be fair yet. Here’s the simpler/safer path I’d recommend first.”

## Post-setup ask

After a successful setup:

> “If this made AI feel safer or easier for your family, is there one person who might appreciate the same free fit check?”
