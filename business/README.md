# Offline Helper Business Development

This folder turns the Offline Helper pilot into a repeatable sales and delivery motion.

## Current offer ladder

1. **Free fit check** — 10–15 minutes, no payment, confirms device/goals/privacy fit.
2. **Starter Setup — $149 pilot** — one person, one device, one first workflow.
3. **Family Setup — $249 pilot** — caregiver/family organizer included, consent and handoff sheet.
4. **Family Support — $29/month optional** — only offered after value is proven.

## Ideal first customer profiles

- Adult child helping a parent/grandparent use AI safely.
- Older adult writing memoirs, letters, genealogy notes, or family stories.
- Family caregiver who wants private help drafting routines, summaries, or reminders.
- Community helper at a church, library, senior center, or nonprofit.
- Privacy-conscious Mac/Windows owner who wants AI without account complexity.

## 30-day business development sprint

### Week 1 — Proof and trust

- Publish 3 plain-English examples: memoir helper, family tech helper, caregiving admin helper.
- Ask 5 warm contacts for feedback on the free fit-check flow.
- Create a simple referral note for adult children and community helpers.

### Week 2 — Community distribution

- Post helpful, non-sales answers in senior tech, caregiver, privacy, Mac, and writing communities.
- Offer the fit check only when the problem clearly matches.
- Track objections verbatim: privacy, price, remote access, computer age, family consent.

### Week 3 — Partner outreach

- Contact 10 libraries, senior centers, churches, writing groups, and local tech helpers.
- Offer a free 20-minute mini-session: “Private AI on your own computer: what families should know.”
- Ask for one pilot referral, not a broad partnership.

### Week 4 — Conversion and retention

- Review every fit check and checkout abandonment.
- Improve one page section based on repeated confusion.
- Invite successful customers to optional Family Support after a clear win.

## Metrics to review weekly

- Fit-check requests
- Qualified fits
- Paid setup conversions
- Top 3 customer goals
- Top 3 objections
- Setup completion time
- Support questions after setup
- Referrals requested / referrals received
