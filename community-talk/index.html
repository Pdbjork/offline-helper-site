20-minute outline

  1. What “local-first AI” means: what can run on a Mac or Windows PC, and what may still use the internet.
  2. Seven family safety checks: consent, passwords, scams, medical/legal/financial boundaries, and human override.
  3. First safe jobs: memoirs, kind reply drafts, grocery planning, plain-English explanations, and questions to ask a qualified human.
  4. When not to pay: unclear consent, device mismatch, crisis needs, secret-sharing pressure, or no useful first job.
  5. Optional next step: free fit check, $99 rescue when stuck today, or the free community for general questions.

Privacy rule: the session does not collect private documents, account passwords, recovery keys, medical/legal/financial details, or crisis stories.
