The seven-point family AI safety check

  1. Consent first: the person using AI understands what is being set up and can say no, pause, or ask for help without pressure.
  2. No secret sharing: nobody pastes passwords, recovery keys, bank details, medical records, legal documents, Social Security numbers, or private family files into a chatbot.
  3. Local vs. cloud is explained: everyone knows which parts run on the home computer and which parts may use the internet, phone messaging, email, cloud backup, or third-party APIs.
  4. Scam boundary is clear: AI does not verify strangers, investment offers, gift-card requests, romance messages, tech-support popups, or emergency money asks.
  5. First useful job is simple: start with one safe task: memoir writing, kind reply drafts, grocery planning, plain-English explanations, study help, or organizing thoughts.
  6. Support access can turn off: any remote-support tool has a clear start/stop moment, and the loved one knows who can see the screen.
  7. Human override stays in charge: AI never makes medical, legal, financial, emergency, or crisis decisions. It can draft questions to ask a qualified human.

Good first AI jobs for families

  • Turn a memory into a memoir chapter outline.
  • Draft a kind text or email, then let the human edit before sending.
  • Explain confusing letters in plain English without uploading sensitive details.
  • Create a weekly grocery or appointment checklist.
  • Make a “questions to ask the doctor / lawyer / advisor” list without asking AI to decide.
