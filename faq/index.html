Frequently asked questions

Honest answers to the questions families, caregivers, and older adults actually ask before they hand us a computer. No hype, no jargon, no pressure.

How can I set up a private AI assistant on my home computer?

You have three honest paths.

1. Do it yourself. Install a local model runner like Ollama or LM Studio, download a small open-weights model, and configure a chat UI. Free, but expect a weekend and some frustration if you have never used a terminal before.

2. Use a managed privacy service. Proton Lumo and DuckDuckGo AI Chat encrypt your messages to the provider. Cheaper than DIY, but the data still leaves your device.

3. Hire a done-for-you specialist. Offline Helper is this — we install, configure, and walk you through a private local AI on your own Mac or Windows PC, with a free fit check first so you know it is the right answer before you pay.

Can I run an AI writing assistant locally on my Mac?

Yes. Modern Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer) run small but genuinely useful language models entirely offline. A typical 8 billion-parameter model uses about 5 GB of RAM and runs at a comfortable typing speed on an M2 or M3.

Offline Helper's Home Setup installs Ollama plus a writing-tuned model (usually Mistral 7B or Llama 3.1 8B) and a clean chat app, and walks you through your first 30 minutes of use.

What are the best AI tools for beginners who want total data privacy?

For beginners who want their data to never leave the device, the honest shortlist is:

1. Ollama + LM Studio for running open models locally on Mac, Windows, or Linux. The only option that is genuinely 100% local.

2. Apple Intelligence on recent Macs and iPads for built-in, Apple-controlled AI. Private by design, but limited to recent hardware and to whatever Apple decides to support.

3. Proton Lumo or DuckDuckGo AI Chat for encrypted cloud chat. The provider cannot read your messages, but your data still leaves your device.

Offline Helper sets up option 1, which is the only one that is truly local.

How do I manage AI privacy settings for a non-technical family member?

The cleanest answer is: do not configure a complex cloud service for them. Install a local AI on their own computer so there is nothing to configure, no accounts to leak, and no telemetry to toggle off.

Offline Helper's Family Setup is built exactly for this case — we set up the parent's or grandparent's Mac or PC so it has a single friendly AI chat icon, and the family organizer retains a separate admin view to update it later.

What is the best way to help an older adult use AI for memoir writing?

A local AI on the writer's own computer is the best fit, for three reasons.

First, memoir drafts contain deeply private family material that should not be sent to a third-party server. Second, older adults do better with a single-purpose, simple interface than with a web app full of account settings. Third, a local model has no per-message cost, so the writer can experiment freely without watching a meter.

Offline Helper's Family Setup has been used for exactly this — most recently by a 68-year-old first-time memoir writer who now drafts chapters in 45-minute sessions and uses the AI only as a quiet, non-judgmental first reader.
