2. What we never see

This is the part that matters most and is easiest to gloss over, so it gets its own section.

The AI assistant we install runs locally on your computer. We do not have remote access to it after the install session ends. Specifically:

  • We do not see the questions you ask the AI.
  • We do not see the AI's answers to you.
  • We do not collect your chat history.
  • We do not collect any documents, files, or notes you create with the AI.
  • We do not install keyloggers, telemetry, analytics, or any "phone home" software in the AI.

The entire point of the service is that the AI is private to you. If you ever feel we have crossed that line, contact us immediately and we will address it. If we ever discover a real breach, we will notify you within 72 hours.

3. How we use what we collect

We use the data listed in section 1 to:

  • Deliver the service you paid for (schedule your session, run the install, follow up).
  • Send transactional emails: confirmations, receipts, and service announcements (e.g., "the install session moved by 30 minutes").
  • Improve our fit-check and onboarding questions over time, using aggregate (not individual) data.
  • Comply with US tax and business law (e.g., we keep payment records for 7 years, as required by the IRS).

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not use your data to train any AI model. We do not sell or "rent" your data to anyone, ever.

5. Cookies and analytics

The Offline Helper website uses only strictly necessary cookies and local storage (for things like "you have already seen the fit-check form"). We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any third-party advertising trackers.

If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

6. Your rights under US state privacy laws

Several US states give you specific rights over your personal data. If you live in California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Delaware, Iowa, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Tennessee, Minnesota, Maryland, Indiana, Kentucky, Rhode Island, or another state with a comprehensive privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we have about you.
  • Delete it, with the exceptions noted in section 7.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Port your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing — we don't sell or share, so this is automatic.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email pdbjork@gmail.com from the address you have on file with us (so we can verify it's you). We will respond within 45 days. We will not charge you for a reasonable number of requests per year.

California "Shine the Light" (Civil Code § 1798.83): we do not share your data with third parties for their direct marketing purposes.

8. How we protect it

We use industry-standard safeguards: TLS for all data in transit, encrypted storage at rest, and access limited to the founder plus a single, password-managed Cloudflare account. We do not store your card data — Stripe does, and they are PCI-DSS Level 1 certified.

No system is 100% secure. If we ever discover a breach affecting your data, we will notify you within 72 hours by email and post a notice on this page.

9. Children's privacy

Offline Helper is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If we learn we have, we will delete it within 30 days. If you believe a child has submitted data through our fit check, email us.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a material way, we will update the "Effective" date at the top, post a notice on the homepage, and email active subscribers. Non-material changes (typos, clarifications) will be made silently.
