# Offline Helper — Brand Card (paste source)

**One-liner:** Private AI setup and tech rescue for families — in plain English, without the shame.

**Positioning:** Offline Helper helps non-technical people (and the relatives who love them) get a safe, private, local-first AI helper working on their own Mac or Windows PC — carefully, patiently, and without pushing cloud accounts they don’t understand.
